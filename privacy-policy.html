Privacy Policy

App Name: ARISE: Daily Workout Exercise
Effective Date: November 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect & Local Storage

ARISE: Daily Workout Exercise is designed with your privacy first. Your workout logs, daily quests, step counts, preferences, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

2. Permissions We Use & Why

  • Location (Foreground & Background): Used to track your running route, distance, pace, and elevation gain during active GPS workouts—including when the screen is locked or the app is in the background. Location data is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Activity Recognition (Physical Activity): Used to count your daily steps accurately using your device's hardware step sensor.
  • Camera: Used only when you choose to take or upload a profile/progress photo inside the app.
  • Biometrics (Fingerprint / Face / PIN): Used solely for the optional in-app security lock feature via Android's native BiometricPrompt. We never access or store your biometric data.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used to deliver workout reminders, daily alarms, and active GPS tracking status notifications.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

Optional VIP subscriptions (ARISE Sovereign VIP) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card, UPI, or banking details. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in Google Play Store settings.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play Services (Location & Billing) and public map tile providers (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, ArcGIS) to display workout maps. Their respective privacy policies govern map tile and billing requests.

5. Data Deletion

Because all workout and preference data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all your data at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
